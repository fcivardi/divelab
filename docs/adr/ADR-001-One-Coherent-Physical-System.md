# ADR-001 — One Coherent Physical System

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-08-01

## Context

Traditional courses switch physical systems from chapter to chapter.

## Decision

Teach the entire curriculum through a scuba diver underwater.

## Consequences

### Positive
- Continuous intuition and stronger narrative.

### Trade-offs
- Some classical examples are omitted or discussed only for comparison.

## Educational Impact

Students build intuition once and deepen it throughout the course.
