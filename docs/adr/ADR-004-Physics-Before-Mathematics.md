# ADR-004 — Physics Before Mathematics

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-08-01

## Context

Equations without intuition are hard to retain.

## Decision

Introduce every mathematical model only after physical motivation.

## Consequences

### Positive
- Improved conceptual understanding.

### Trade-offs
- Chapters may be longer.

## Educational Impact

Concepts are understood before formal derivations.
