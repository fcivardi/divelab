# ADR-005 — Human in the Loop

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-08-01

## Context

The diver remains part of the control system.

## Decision

Treat the diver as the controller whenever appropriate.

## Consequences

### Positive
- Bridges classical control and human factors.

### Trade-offs
- Human behaviour is harder to model.

## Educational Impact

Shows how engineering applies to real people.
