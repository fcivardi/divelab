# ADR-003 — Book and Notebooks

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-08-01

## Context

Books explain ideas while notebooks encourage experimentation.

## Decision

The book is the narrative; notebooks are executable laboratories.

## Consequences

### Positive
- Clear separation of theory and experimentation.

### Trade-offs
- Requires maintaining two complementary artefacts.

## Educational Impact

Readers alternate naturally between reading and experimentation.
